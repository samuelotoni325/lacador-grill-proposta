Questions

How does the buffet by weight work?

Serve yourself, take your plate to the scale at the register and pay by the weight of the food. The plate’s weight is deducted automatically.

How much per pound?

Monday to Thursday, $15.99. Friday, weekends and holidays, $16.99.

Can I order à la carte?

Yes. There are 13 fixed-price executive plates and picanha boards at the Laçador window, to dine in or take out.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking at Lake Buena Vista Factory Stores.

Do you cater events?

Yes, with a custom menu, at Laçador’s space or at your venue.
